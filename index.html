How to Play Explora

Explora puts you inside an underground cave where light cannot reach. Instead of relying on sight, you explore using sound and a refreshable tactile grid.

Keyboard Shortcuts (Eyes-Free Play)

  • Up / W — Step North
  • Down / S — Step South
  • Left / A — Step West
  • Right / D — Step East
  • Space / E — Emit Echo Pulse
  • R — Repeat Last Spoken Prompt
  • I / L — Speak Position & Status
  • F — Feel Current Tile & Hints
  • M — Toggle Tactile Memory Mode
  • Esc — Return to Main Menu

Understanding Echo Pulses

When you press Space, your explorer sends out a sonar wave. After the tactile display refreshes its physical pins, you will hear directional audio cues (panned left for West, right for East) and a concise voice summary:

  • Metallic Chime: A hidden treasure is nearby! The chime is higher and brighter when closer.
  • Rhythmic Heartbeat: A patrolling Cave Beetle is nearby. Wait a step or walk around it!
  • Resonant Portal Chord: The Cavern Exit is within range. Collect all treasures first to unlock it.
